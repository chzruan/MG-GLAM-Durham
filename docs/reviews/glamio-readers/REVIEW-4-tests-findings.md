## 1. Summary

Reproduced the author's numbers: the suite passes 221/221 in 38.5 s wall (82 MB RSS, 4
threads) with all six `glamio:` verdicts PASS (3 schedule rows; 3 lens outputs; 12 cubes;
6 PowerDM files; 3 subsample outputs with their PMcrd/PMcrs; 2 fR_halo outputs, 6 rows), and
the unit tests pass 8/8 in script mode and 15/15 under pytest -- but only *without* `-I`:
the documented `python3 -I -m pytest -q tests` fails with `No module named pytest` because
pytest lives only in the user site (`~/.local`), which `-I` excludes (R4-2). A battery of 27
mutants (the 18 requested plus 9 extra) shows the two layers are complementary: 22 are caught
by at least one layer, the suite alone misses 12, the unit tests alone miss 6, and 5 escape
both (physical-position wrap, exact-multiple page count, the order of header fields other
than aexpn/istep/ngrid/nparticles/box, the sidecar `SALT` parse). The snapshot fixture
builds its header with the reader's own `_header_dtype`, so a field-order error in
`_FIELDS` is invisible to the unit tests (R4-3, Major). Probing the untested empty-product
path found a real defect: `mmap=True` on a legitimate `rows = 0` fR_halo (the Fortran writes
the empty `.bin` and the sidecar) raises numpy's `cannot mmap an empty file` (R4-1, Major).
The oracles are independent where it matters (ProjectDENSIT, PowerDM.R, the decoded-particle
reconstruction, the PMcrs-vs-subsample quanta check); the raw reshape/decode formulas are
necessarily shared and I say so below. Merge recommendation: **ready after the listed
fixes** (R4-1, R4-3 and R4-9 before merging; the suite blind spots R4-4..R4-8 can follow
as a test-only commit).
